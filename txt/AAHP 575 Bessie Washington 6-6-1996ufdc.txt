AAHP 575 Bessie Washington 6-6-1996 Interviewed by Vivian Hill on June 6, 1996 60 minutes | 34 pages



Abstract: In this interview, Fort Meyers elder Bessie Washington recalls her life as the youngest of twelve children growing up in various cities in Central Florida. Washington was born in Gretna, Florida, but moved to Lake Wales, where her father helped design the Bok Tower. After her father’s death, Washington and her family moved to Bradenton, then Sarasota, where Washington met her first husband. After the death of her first husband, Washington moved to Fort Meyers where she met her second husband. Later, she and her second husband moved to Brooklyn, New York, where they managed rental properties. Washington recalls her careers as a beautician and seamstress, noting that she had to retire after her breast cancer diagnosis. Although she had to endure a double mastectomy, Washington survived her battle with cancer and went on to retire in Fort Meyers, Florida.



Keywords: [Fort Meyers, Saint Petersburg, Sarasota, Gretna, Quincy, Brooklyn, New York, Central Florida, Lake Wales, Black community, Jones Walker Hospital]
