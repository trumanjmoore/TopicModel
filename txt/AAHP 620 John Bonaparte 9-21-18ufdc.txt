AAHP 620 John Bonaparte 9-21-2018 Interviewed by Kendriana Drayton on September 21, 2018

45 minutes | 21 pages



Abstract: In this interview John Bonaparte’s granddaughter sits down with him to discuss his life in Central Florida and his careers in the Navy, as a newspaper printer, and as a carpenter. Bonaparte, who served in World War II on supply convoys bringing food to various bases around the globe, reflects on segregation in the military. He also reflects on the effects of segregation and desegregation alike in civilian life.





Keywords: [Palatka, Florida, Black community, Jacksonville, military, Navy, World War II, WWII, segregation]
