AAHP 016 Ethel Williams

African American History Project (AAHP) Interview conducted by Ann Smith on March 2, 2010

1 hour 8 minutes | 44 pages



Abstract: In this interview, Ethel Williams primarily describes her family and memories of the cities she lived in. She was an only child who was raised by her grandfather and mother in Perry, Florida. She loved school and reading, and was sent to boarding school in Live Oak for high school because there were schoolhouses being burned near Perry. As a young adult, she loved to go dances in Jacksonville, and met her husband at one of them. They ended up living in Shamrock and had a daughter together. Mrs.

Williams said that her proudest accomplishments in life were her mother and daughter. Both of them had passed away by the time of this interview, but her mother lived to be one hundred years old. She hopes that she will also live to be a hundred. Her advice to living a long life is to do the right thing because everything will come to you.



Keywords: [African American History; Taylor County, Florida; Alachua County, Florida]
