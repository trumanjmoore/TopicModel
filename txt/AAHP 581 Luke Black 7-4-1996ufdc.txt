AAHP 581 Luke Black Interviewed by Unidentified on July, 4, 1996

39 minutes | 23 pages



Abstract: In this interview, Elder Luke Black recounts his experiences growing up in Chipley, Florida, as well as his life in Quincy, Florida and Fort Myers, Florida. These experiences include his work in tobacco farming as a young person, as well as preaching and eventually becoming a pastor in Fort Myers. Elder Black also reflects on changes he has witnessed in Fort Myers over the years, as well as the role of religion in his community, and the relationship between the African-American and White communities in Chipley and Fort Myers.



Keywords: [Chipley, Florida; Fort Myers, Florida; farming; race relations; preaching; pastors; faith; African American History]
