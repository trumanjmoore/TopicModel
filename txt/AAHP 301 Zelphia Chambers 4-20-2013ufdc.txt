AAHP 301 Zelphia Chambers

African American History Project (AAHP)

Interviewed by Justin Dunnavant and Sherry Dupree on April 20, 2013 53 minutes | 38 pages



Abstract: Ms. Zelphia Chambers, along with Harland Mitchell and Mary Cummings, is being interviewed by Sherry Dupree and Justin Dunnavant in Starke, Florida on April 20, 2013. The interview begins with a discussion of Ms. Chambers’s family members.

Ms. Chambers then describes the education opportunities in the town, as well as the religious institutions. Ms. Chambers discusses her involvement on her family’s farm. There is conversation about the local turpentine industry.



Keywords: Starke; Bradford County; turpentine; farmer
