AAHP 569 JC Reed

African American History Project (AAHP) Interviewed by Michael Parker on June 1, 2018 45 minutes | 31 pages



Abstract: Mr. Reed, a decorated veteran, is being interviewed by SPOHP volunteer Michael Parker on June 1, 2018. Mr. Reed starts talking about his life of growing up in McIntosh, Alabama during the Depression, getting drafted into the military for World War II being a part of D-Day working as a stove mechanic at that time. After his return, he settled down and lived his life being a life insurance agent, a single father of nine children, and living as a Black man during and after segregation in the south. He dealt with being part of the federal grand jury of the events of Bloody Sunday 1965 and his close encounter with the Ku Klux Klan.



Keywords: [Segregation, Integration, Bloody Sunday, Ku Klux Klan, World War II, Pearl Harbor, D-Day, Normandy, Depression, Private First Class, 338th Engineer Regiment, Stove Mechanic, Life Insurance Agent, Sawmill.]
