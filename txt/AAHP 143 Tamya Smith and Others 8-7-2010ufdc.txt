AAHP 143 Tamya Smith and Maurice Lewis African American History Project (AAHP)

Interview conducted by Anna Brodrecht on August 7, 2010 9 minutes | 9 pages



Abstract: This was recorded with several of the children present at a family reunion where Isaiah Branton had invited SPOHP to conduct oral history interviews. The children speak about their families, and some of their preferred recreational activities, while also learning how to use the Marantz recorder the team was using for the interviews.
